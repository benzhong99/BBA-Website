What does “Smart SPA Total Solution” include?

The concept combines a smart comfort system, privacy-conscious sensing, compatible wearable options, experience software, loyalty and rewards, and an operations platform.

Can businesses manage multiple stations?

Yes. The business concept includes appointments, multi-station status, customer preferences, utilization, equipment status, loyalty progress, and de-identified operational trends.

Is Smart SPA a medical device?

The concept is positioned for general wellness and relaxation only. It is not intended for diagnosis, treatment, cure, mitigation, prevention, clinical decision-making, or emergency response.

Are the prices and payments live?

The prices and checkout in this prototype are illustrative. Final commercial prices require approval, and production payment must be connected to a secure, PCI-compliant hosted payment provider before launch.
