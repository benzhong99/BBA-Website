What is included in the 90-day pilot?

The $2,580 per-station monthly fee covers hardware, software, and managed service. The $1,199 onboarding fee includes on-site delivery, professional installation, and initial staff training. Final scope is confirmed in the signed agreement.

When does the 90-day pilot begin?

The pilot begins after clinical review of participant eligibility and device documentation, review of data practices, installation, staff training, and center acceptance. Shipping time does not count toward the 90-day pilot period; delivery, installation, pilot, and payment dates are defined in the signed agreement.

What will the pilot evaluate?

The pilot can evaluate attendance, retention, enrollment, participant satisfaction, staff time, and net contribution after additional costs. Attendance and acquisition results are reviewed against the center’s own baseline.

Does the center own the equipment?

No deposit is required and BBA owns the equipment. The signed agreement defines any continued service, early termination, when billing ends, equipment removal costs, and data export or return.

Is Smart SPA a medical device or emergency monitor?

The offering is positioned for general wellness and relaxation. It is not intended for diagnosis, treatment, clinical decision-making, or emergency monitoring.

How are participant privacy and data handled?

The center and BBA review data practices, permissions, roles, and responsibilities before launch. The intended architecture keeps identity and eligibility information under center control while minimizing information in BBA’s operations layer.

Can I buy the home version online?

Not yet. Home availability and pricing have not been announced. Use the inquiry form to request future information.
